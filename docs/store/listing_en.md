## Full description (max 4000 chars)

"Moped & Motorcycle License Kore!" is a study app focused on the written test for two-wheeled licenses in Japan. The questions and explanations are in Japanese. It covers moped (gentsuki), small-displacement standard motorcycle, standard motorcycle, large motorcycle and AT-restricted license categories.

MAIN FEATURES
- Question sets by license category: questions and explanations for every category are free
- Daily quota: work through a small daily goal
- Mock tests: practice in a test-like format
- Readiness meter: a rough indicator of your progress based on your answers
- Weak-area analysis and review
- Study plan up to your exam date
- Traffic sign quizzes and tricky-question practice

NEW
- Hazard prediction scenarios: choose how to react in situations specific to riding a motorcycle
- Skill test tips: reading material and practice checklists for the one-plank bridge, slalom, emergency braking, S-curve/crank and turning around
- Bike encyclopedia: introductions to fictional bike types that unlock as you study
- Color display of how much you have solved: see at a glance how much you have worked through (it shows the amount of practice, not whether answers were right)

PRICING
The app is free to start, and all questions and explanations are free. There are two optional one-time purchases:
- Ad-free: removes ads
- Premium: ad-free, plus unlimited mock tests, section scores, full weak-area analysis, a study plan to your exam date, and hazard scenarios from the third onward

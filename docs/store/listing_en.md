## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Need to pass the written test in a short time? Tricky questions keep tripping you up? Can't yet picture the dangerous moments that are specific to riding a motorcycle?

"Moped & Motorcycle License Kore!" is built for exactly that: the written test for Japan's moped and motorcycle licenses. The questions and explanations are in Japanese. It covers moped (gentsuki), small-displacement standard motorcycle, standard motorcycle, large motorcycle and AT-restricted categories, and the questions and explanations for every category are free.

WHAT IT HELPS WITH

Short on time
- Set your exam date to see the days left (Premium also shows a daily target worked back from your exam date)
- A daily quota gives you one clear thing to do each day
- Mock tests let you try a test-like format

Not sure how far you have come
- See how much of each category and topic you have worked through as a color, in 5 levels from 0% to 100%. Pale topics are where to go next. (It shows the amount of practice, not whether answers were right.)
- Weak-area analysis and review show where you slip

Tricky questions
- Tricky-question practice, traffic sign quizzes and a number-memorizing quiz

Situations specific to two wheels
- Hazard prediction scenarios: experience a situation and choose how to react, then read why. The first 2 are free; the rest are Premium
- Skill test tips: reading material and practice checklists for the one-plank bridge, slalom, emergency braking, S-curve/crank and turning around (open it from "Useful info" on the home screen)

A reason to keep going
- Bike encyclopedia: introductions to fictional bike types that unlock as you study. They are not modeled on real models. (Open it from Settings.)

FEATURES AT A GLANCE
- Question sets by license category, with explanations (free)
- Daily quota
- Mock tests
- Readiness meter: a rough indicator based on your answers
- Color display of how much you have solved (5 levels)
- Weak-area analysis and review
- Hazard prediction scenarios
- Skill test tips
- Bike encyclopedia
- Exam date countdown

PRICING
The app is free to start, and all questions and explanations are free. There are two optional one-time purchases:
- Ad-free: removes ads
- Premium: ad-free, plus unlimited mock tests, section scores, full weak-area analysis, a study plan to your exam date, and hazard scenarios from the third onward
Prices are shown on the Google Play purchase screen. [Ops to confirm: prices are provisional; do not state amounts here as final]
Purchases can be restored.

ADS
The free version shows a banner ad on the home screen and an interstitial ad when you finish your daily quota (your daily study goal). No ads are shown on the question, boss battle, or pass-prediction meter screens.
Ads can be removed with either purchase.

PLEASE NOTE
- This app is a study aid. It does not guarantee that you will pass any test or achieve any result.
- Traffic rules and test content can change with legal revisions, so questions and explanations in the app may be out of date. Always check the latest information from public authorities such as the National Police Agency, your prefectural police and your driver's license center.
- This app is not affiliated with or provided by any public authority or driving school. [Ops to confirm]
- Some questions, explanations and scenarios have not yet been reviewed by a specialist (this includes AI-assisted drafts). Such material is labeled inside the app. [Ops to confirm: review status at release]

PRIVACY
You can sign in with a Google account to keep your progress across devices, or use the app offline with data kept only on your device. The app uses Firebase Analytics (usage measurement), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports), Google AdMob (ads) and RevenueCat (purchase management). You can choose your ad consent on the first-launch screen (Google UMP). See the privacy policy for details.
